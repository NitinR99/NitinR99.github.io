# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit this website or contact me, as required by Art. 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Who is responsible

Nitin Ramesh, [nramesh11a@gmail.com](mailto:nramesh11a@gmail.com) (see also the [legal notice](/legal-notice)).

## In short

- No cookies, analytics, tracking, advertising or social-media plugins.
- Fonts, images and scripts are served from this website itself. No content is loaded from third parties such as Google Fonts.
- The only personal data involved is what the hosting provider logs when your browser requests a page, and what you send me by email.

## Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Pages are delivered through GitHub's content delivery network, which may serve you from a location in Europe.

When you open a page, your browser sends technical data that GitHub records, in particular:

- IP address
- date and time of the request
- requested page, browser type and operating system
- referrer URL (the page you came from)

GitHub uses this data to deliver the website and to keep the service secure, for example against attacks. I do not have access to these logs and do not use them.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is offering this website reliably and securely.
- **Transfer to the USA:** GitHub may process the data in the USA. GitHub bases such transfers on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **Retention:** determined by GitHub. Details are in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Contact by email

If you email me, I process your email address, name and whatever else you include, in order to reply. My emails are handled by Google (Gmail), which processes them on its servers, including outside the EU. Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering your message), or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your message concerns a job opportunity or another potential contract.
- **Retention:** I delete the conversation once it is finished and there is no reason to keep it, for example an ongoing application process.

## Theme setting

This website saves your light/dark mode preference in your browser's local storage (`vitepress-theme-appearance`), so the page displays in the right theme. It holds only `auto`, `light` or `dark`, stays on your device and is never sent to me or anyone else. It is strictly necessary for displaying the website as you requested, so no consent is required (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time through your browser settings.

## Links to other websites

This website links to other sites, such as LinkedIn. No data is shared with them until you click a link. From then on, their own privacy policies apply.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal data I hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
- have it **corrected** (Art. 16) or **deleted** (Art. 17)
- **restrict** its processing (Art. 18)
- receive it in a portable format (**data portability**, Art. 20)
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21)

To exercise any of these, email me.

You also have the right to **lodge a complaint** with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state where you live or work. German authorities are listed by the [Federal Commissioner for Data Protection (BfDI)](https://www.bfdi.bund.de/DE/Service/Anschriften/Laender/Laender-node.html).

No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

## Changes

I update this policy when the website or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
